Cass by Sabb Technologies

Use information

Cass is a personal assistant installation for its configured owner. It is not a public account-connection service. It assists with requests within its implemented tools and authority rules.

Email drafts are created for review in Gmail. Approving a send authorizes the specific prepared message, and editing that draft can require a fresh preview. Confirm recipients and content before approving external communication.

AI-generated responses and drafts can be incomplete or mistaken. The owner remains responsible for reviewing proposed actions and their results. The integration may be interrupted by revoked authorization, provider changes, errors, or unavailable services.

The owner may disconnect Cass’s Google access through Google Account settings. This page explains the installation’s operation; the separate agreements for Google, Slack, AWS, and OpenAI continue to govern those services.
