Cass by Sabb Technologies

Privacy policy

Prepared October 6, 2026. This policy describes the personal Cass installation.

Google access and purpose

Cass requests Gmail read access and permission to create drafts and send email. Gmail search results, selected message contents and headers, recipients, draft text, and send receipts are used to provide the owner’s requested assistant features. Saving a draft does not send it. Sending requires the owner’s approval of the prepared message.

Processing and storage

The Slack and account integration runs in Amazon Web Services. Requested email content and task data may be sent to the existing OpenAI API model to understand a request and prepare a response or draft. Relevant conversation data, action previews, task snapshots, and draft content may be stored in AWS conversation and action records. Gmail drafts and sent messages are stored in Gmail.

Google authorization credentials are stored in AWS Secrets Manager and used by the account worker. Credentials are not included in the source repository or sent to the model as conversation content. Application checks restrict personal account tools to the configured owner in Cass’s direct-message conversation.

Retention and control

Some action records have expiry times and asynchronous cleanup. Conversation records may remain until the owner removes them. Credentials remain until the owner revokes or removes the connection. The owner can review, edit, or remove messages and drafts in Gmail and revoke Cass’s Google connection in Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future account access; it does not automatically delete existing Gmail items or AWS records.

Disclosure

Google user data is used for the requested personal assistant features, not sold or used by this application for advertising. AWS, OpenAI, Slack, and Google process relevant data as part of their services. Human access to stored application data depends on the owner’s account and administrator permissions. This page does not promise that provider systems or administrators can never access data.

Contact

Use the developer support address displayed on Cass’s Google consent screen.
