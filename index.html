Cass

Cass is a personal Chief of Staff assistant operated through Slack. This Google connection lets the owner request Gmail searches, read selected messages, create email drafts, and approve sending a prepared draft.

Requested drafts are saved in Gmail. Sending requires the owner’s approval of the prepared message. Personal account operations are restricted by the application to the configured owner’s Cass direct-message conversation.

This is a personal installation, not a public service or an invitation to connect another person’s mailbox.
